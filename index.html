NoAdsDude

Websites that do the thing. Nothing else.

NoAdsDude is a handful of small websites built without ads, popups, cookie banners, tracking scripts, newsletter traps, or "create an account to continue."

No analytics. No creepy pixels. No autoplay videos. No dark patterns. No waiting for twelve different ad networks before you can check the weather, read a recipe, or look up a score.

Maybe this is the future of the internet. Maybe it's just the part of the old internet worth keeping. Either way, I got tired of bloated pages that treat people like inventory, so I started making alternatives.

Why make this?

Because most websites got weird.

A weather page should not need a consent manager, six trackers, a video ad, and half your battery.

A recipe should not make you scroll through a memoir and a stack of affiliate links.

A health article should not be wrapped in panic headlines, miracle-product funnels, or "doctors hate this one trick" garbage.

The goal here is simple: make pages that load fast, respect your privacy, and get out of the way.

The rules

No ads.
Not "fewer ads." Not "better ads." None.
No tracking.
No analytics, cookies, fingerprinting, pixels, or third-party surveillance scripts.
No engagement traps.
No infinite scroll, no newsletter wall, no fake urgency, no "you may also like" maze.
One job per site.
Each site should do the thing it says on the label, then leave you alone.

Privacy

This page makes zero third-party requests. No analytics, no cookies, no fingerprinting, no remote fonts, no embedded widgets. Fonts and stylesheets are served from noadsdude.com.

The only thing stored locally is your theme preference, kept in your browser's localStorage. That is not a cookie.

Outbound links to sibling sites (noadsweather.com, noadssports.com, backonthemenu.com, noadsphotos.com, partnermassage.app, noadshealth.com) and to Ko-fi take you off this site; their own privacy practices apply once you click.
